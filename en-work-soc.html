Where it started

Registration, lookups and review dates were scattered.

Every IP has a reason for investigation, a ticket, a duration and a review date. When these are spread across files, finding history and tracking dates becomes repetitive work.

Batch lookups and tracking in one app

The app retrieves IP information in batches, suggests decisions using rules and tracks records through review and release confirmation. Editable decisions and history stay with the record.

From intake to reports

Batch intake, custom rules and detailed reports

Open any section to view its image.

One IP or a whole list

Paste a list or import a file. The app validates inputs, retrieves IP information and suggests a decision for each row. You can edit it before registration.

Reports alongside the IP

See the abuse score, report counts, last report and network information together. Retrieved AbuseIPDB report details can also be viewed and copied.

Your rules, more consistent decisions

Define rules for country, score, usage type or other conditions: a duration, skip registration or require a manual decision. You control order and exceptions.

Find an IP’s record

Search by IP, ticket or scenario. See status, duration, registration and review dates together, then open details and history.

Combine multiple conditions

For example, find blocked records with a specific scenario. Select fields, operators and values, and narrow by country, score or date range.

Keep track of review dates

Due records are collected in the review queue. Check fresh information and extend the duration or queue the record for release. Final confirmation is a separate step.

Batch lookup without registering records

If you only need to investigate a list, retrieve its information and export to Excel. Results, retrieved reports and errors are included.

Dates in your preferred calendar

Choose the Jalali or Gregorian calendar for date ranges and review dates. Filtered records can also be exported.

Automated checks, editable decisions

Automated lookups

Avoid collecting the same information again.

Batch lookup and rule-based duration suggestions replace repeating the same steps for each row.

Editable decisions

Suggestions stay under your control.

Edit each row’s country and duration, and define exceptions or require manual decisions.

Visible follow-up

From review date to final confirmation.

Extension, pending release and final confirmation have explicit states. This app manages records and workflow; it doesn’t enforce firewall blocks.
