It started with a specific purchase

Gooshiinoo was looking for used iPhones.

The shop checked iPhone listings in Tehran for purchases. Results included stores, installment offers, buyers and replicas; identifying them meant repeatedly reading descriptions.

We gave the repetitive work to the bot.

Search, check titles and descriptions, exclude unsuitable listings and keep processing history. The result is more consistent tracking with less time spent on irrelevant ads.
