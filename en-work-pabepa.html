PabePa · A bot and mini app for shared habits

Make your plan.
Move forward together.

Stop rewriting recurring plans or losing outcomes among messages. PabePa brings daily and weekly plans, both players’ progress and friendly competition together, with teammate messages and optional calorie tracking.

For everyday tasks

From making a plan to seeing progress.

Open a section to see the product’s actual interface.

Set up recurring tasks once

Turn reading, exercise or another habit into a mission that stays on your daily plan.

Set goals for today

List today’s tasks separately from ongoing habits to give the day a clear plan.

Choose your days of the week

Schedule the gym for Saturday and Monday, for example. The task appears on those days automatically.

Record the outcome with a tap

Done, not done or skipped today: keep task outcomes and progress clear.

Describe your food for a calorie estimate

Describe what you ate in your own words. Review and correct the AI estimate before saving it.

Keep daily calories in one place

Log food and compare the daily total with your calorie target. This feature is optional for the team.

For teammates

See each other’s progress. Stay in touch.

Progress together and compete with teams

See your combined score and follow the team’s place in monthly and all-time rankings.

Send your teammate a message or reminder

Write a message or send a reminder from the app. Your teammate receives it in Telegram.

Choose what to share

Control food-detail sharing and reminder permission. Each player changes only their own tasks.

Make the team your own

Invite a teammate, pick a name and badge, and agree on a weekly or monthly reward.
