Privacy

Here’s what information this site collects and what happens to it.

What does the site collect?

There’s no sign-up or user account here. The contact form prepares the name, company, contact details and description you enter. When you press send, your own email app opens with the message ready. You decide whether to send it.

Your display settings, such as light or dark mode and sound on or off, stay in your browser’s storage. They aren’t sent anywhere.

Visitor stats

Visitor analytics help us see which pages people read. The tool records anonymous, combined stats rather than identifying individual visitors.

What you share in our consultation

What you tell me about your business in our consultation stays with me. I won’t use or share it elsewhere. If you need an NDA, we’ll sign one before we look into your work.

Project data

Where possible, your business data stays on your own infrastructure. If we need sample data to build something, we’ll agree on what we need and how long to keep it before we start. If we don’t need a piece of data, we won’t ask for it.
