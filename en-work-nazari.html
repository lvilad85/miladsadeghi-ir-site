From checking prices to choosing gold.

NAZARI GOLD
Prices + catalog + management
For customersFor the shopWhat gets easier?Each role

For the customer

Make a clearer choice before contacting the shop.

Prices, photos and details are accessible, with a contact route for ordering in the same place.

Check prices without asking

See gold, coin and currency prices with an update time. The channel price post updates automatically, reducing the need to wait for a salesperson.

Choose gold with a photo and price

Browse photos, weight, making charges and prices calculated from current rates. Filter by jewellery type or find an item by its code.

Browse by budget and making charge

Choose a budget range to see matching items. A separate making-charge filter helps narrow down the choice.

Get notified at your target price

Set an upper or lower threshold for gold, coins or currency. The bot notifies you when the price reaches it.

Calculate a gold price

Enter weight and a making-charge percentage to calculate against current gold rates. A separate calculator accepts global ounce and dollar prices.

Submit a gold-selling request

Enter the weight and type for an initial estimate. Photos, invoice and contact details complete the request for the shop; the final price follows an in-person assessment.

For the shop

More customer attention, less repetitive work.

Prices, products and sale status stay connected.

Fewer repetitive price enquiries

The bot retrieves rates and updates the channel price post, reducing manual price entry.

The catalog connects to the channel

The shop records the photo, weight and charges. The bot publishes the item to its channel, linking customers to pricing and the ordering route.

Sold listings stay clear

The seller marks the sale. The bot updates the active catalog and channel listing so customers can see that the item is no longer available.

Gold-selling requests reach the manager with the necessary information. Product management and user statistics are in the bot too; the seller handles assessment, advice and finalizing the transaction.
