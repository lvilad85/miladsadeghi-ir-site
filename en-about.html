About Milad Sadeghi

Founder of SmartFlowX

Who I am

I’m Milad Sadeghi, founder of SmartFlowX. I work from Tehran and online with businesses across Iran. Whether we build a chatbot, AI agent or automation, we start with a real example of your daily work.

For several years I’ve been building tools and systems for businesses - from process automation to AI agents. What I’ve always been after is the same thing: finding the spot where work is being done by hand, over and over, and building a system that does that same work faster, more accurately and without getting tired.

Expertise

  • Designing and building AI agents for businesses
  • Automating organizational processes and connecting scattered tools together
  • Building custom chatbots and smart assistants
  • Designing automated reporting and data analysis
  • A strong technical background in networking, servers and infrastructure - the systems I build are reliable and stable

Background

I started out in graphic design, then spent a few years active in the financial markets, and these days my focus is on building smart systems. At every stage, I’ve chased the same thing: building tools to work faster.

Education

BSc in Computer Engineering - Mazandaran University of Science and Technology

Why SmartFlowX

I believe every business can have a faster, smarter version of itself. That’s what SmartFlowX builds - systems that keep your own process, just make it several times faster.
