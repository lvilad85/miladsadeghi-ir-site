Work / Adrina

Adrina Parto · A mobile store in Telegram

Easier shopping for customers.
Simpler management for the store.

Adrina brings product details, budget choices, instalments and buying or selling requests into one bot and storefront. Instalment calculations, price updates and stock notifications are automated, making selection and store management easier.

For customers

From “What fits my budget?” to a request.

Tap “See this screen” to explore the actual interface.

Explore new phones

Review models, storage, colours and purchase options alongside photos and automatically updated prices.

Explore used phones

See photos, video, storage, battery health, registration and the store’s price before getting in touch.

Find phones within your budget

Enter your cash budget or deposit to see suitable phone options.

Calculate instalments before buying

Choose a term and payment method for your selected phone, then see the deposit and monthly payment.

Want to sell your phone?

Enter specifications, condition and photos. The store receives an organised request for review and follow-up.

Can’t find the phone you want?

Submit the model and terms you want so the store knows what to look for.

Find the SIM number you want

Browse numbers and prices, then narrow your choice by search, number code and sorting.

Keep information and follow-up close

Market rates, favourites, new used-phone alerts and store contact are available in the same place.

For the store manager

Inventory, customers and follow-up, together.

Tasks that used to span separate messages and lists now have a clear place.

List once; manage display and alerts together

Add a used phone with media and price. Publish it to the channel and notify subscribed customers.

Organised buying and selling requests

Customer specifications and media are together. Update review status and manage follow-up from the panel.

Keep in touch with customers

Send text, photos, video, audio or files. Select the audience and review a preview before sending.

See customer numbers and activity

See user counts, active users, daily and monthly activity and notification subscribers in one place.

SIM management and Excel import, instalments for a custom amount, market-volatility controls and price management are also part of the same system.
